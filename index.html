How it works

Everything follows 1,000 new riders for 18 months (548 Bounce Days). Results are stated as journey length: the average rental days in one journey (first plan plus renewals, until the EV is returned), at the current exit rate, compared at the same Bounce Day. The retention data (share of riders reaching each Bounce Day) is only the input that exit rates are taken from.
Margin per rental day = rent − energy − maintenance − bike − misc. Margin given back is measured against this.
Cost per grant = cost per use × used %. Cost per rider at a milestone = sum of (benefits given × cost per grant).
Journey length variable: every daily exit rate is divided by (1 + the % entered), so journeys are that much longer or shorter before the programme starts.
Results: the model cuts every exit rate by the same %, until the margin on the extra rental days equals the benefit cost. A cut of X in exit rates is journeys longer by 1 ÷ (1 − X) − 1. Benefits are costed on the longer journeys, so riders the programme keeps also earn benefits.
Every four weeks: the same break-even, counting only costs and rental days up to that week. Early weeks carry costs before most of the extra days arrive, so they need more.
Break-even at each milestone: extra days needed per rider = that milestone's cost per rider ÷ margin on an extra day. Journey length increase needed = those extra days ÷ the days riders reaching that milestone would rent anyway.

Where the numbers come from

Rent ₹295.5 a day: actual rent per paid rental day for riders who started on or after 15 Jan 2026 (to 24 Nov 2026), excluding activation packs, fees and deposits. 7-day plans are 64% of rental days at ₹277 a day, 1-day plans 28% at ₹338, 3-day 5% at ₹333. Costs per rental day: owner's figures: energy ₹80 and maintenance ₹20 while the EV is moving, bike ₹100 a day.
Rider retention: the share of those 38,269 new riders still renting on each Bounce Day (paid rental days), counting every exit, including riders who later come back. Riders still renting on 24 Nov are counted up to that day only. Measured up to Day 271. After that, retention follows the weekly rate in "After the retention data ends" (6.8% by default, the rate seen over Days 183–267). The model runs to Day 548, which is 18 months. Business rules applied; June 2026 checks for submissions, deployments, same-day swaps and renewals reproduced.
Benefit costs and usage: concept doc, with prices from the data. Free day = a day's rent (₹295.5), 90% used. Pay later = 10% of used days unpaid at the 1-day price ₹338 (₹33.8), 30% used. EMI = ₹430 a week lost when a daily rider moves to a 7-day plan (7 × ₹338 − ₹1,938), 17.5% (midpoint of 10–25%). Hold = margin lost on the held day (₹46, still the old estimate), half of hold days used. Goodies at cost, all claimed.
Milestones: one benefit per milestone. Rescue benefits (pay later, hold EV, EMI week) the day before a 7-day renewal, rewards (free day, goodies, gold coins) the day after. Weekly to Day 92, every two weeks to Day 183, every four weeks to Day 548, based on the weekly exit rates in the data.
